Before we start (for a parent)

Tellolo helps a child practise reading aloud. Here is what happens to data:

  • When your child taps record, up to 60 seconds of audio goes to the Tellolo server and on to Meta's Voice Transcribe to turn it into text. Tellolo does not save the audio or the text.
  • Stories and pictures are made by Meta's Muse Spark and Muse Image. Only the language, level, interests and practice words are sent, never your child's name.
  • Your child's first name and reading results (which words were tricky) are saved on the Tellolo server: your own computer if you run Tellolo yourself, or the demo server if you use a web link. They are tied to a random code kept in this browser, so other visitors can't see them.
  • You can delete them at any time under For parents. On the hosted demo they are also deleted automatically after a day.
  • This is a hackathon demo: it has only been tested with adult and computer voices.
